## Lesser Spotted Eagle · 2008

## Little Egret · 2008

A late Little Egret passes in November with a Great Egret.

## Eurasian Skylark · 2008

Passage is strongly dominated by a single day in the second half of October.

## Woodlark · 2008

The season is excellent, ten years after the 1998 record. Passage is concentrated in the first twenty days of October, with several sizeable flocks.

## Osprey · 2008

The season is fairly average. Passage is concentrated in a similar way to that of Western Marsh Harriers. Two observations outside monitoring, including a bird fishing at Étournel, supplement those from the watchpoint.

## European Honey Buzzard · 2008

Passage returns to a reasonable level after a poor 2007 season. Comparisons with other sites suggest that fine weather in late August allowed broad-front migration over the Jura. A second wave, fairly late for the site, appears in early September.

## Hen Harrier · 2008

Passage is average. Without a winter escape movement, it remains very scattered, while several birds are also seen hunting over nearby farmland.

## Montagu's Harrier · 2008

The season is average, with passage centred on late August. The species’ scarcity at the Défilé is linked to small breeding populations northeast of the region.

## Western Marsh Harrier · 2008

Passage is poor and concentrated mainly in early September. Mid-September weather may have shifted some movement eastwards, as observations near Geneva suggest, while few birds pass the Défilé.

## Common Buzzard · 2008

The season is among the strongest since the 2003 record. Passage unfolds in several waves over an extended period, with particularly sustained activity in October.

## White Stork · 2008

Passage increases sharply, as at other sites in the region. Most movement is concentrated from late August to early September. Flocks stop in nearby fields, allowing many rings to be read.

## Black Stork · 2008

After the 2007 record, passage returns to average. An initial summer wave is followed by a late-August peak, then renewed movement in mid-September.

## Short-toed Snake Eagle · 2008

A July bird is probably wandering, whereas September observations are more consistent with migration, matching passage at sites farther south.

## Zitting Cisticola · 2008

A Zitting Cisticola is reported in active migration for the first time at the site, subject to acceptance by the regional rarities committee.

## Rook · 2008

Passage is weak, far below the large concentrations of corvids seen in the past.

## Eurasian Whimbrel · 2008

Birds pass ahead of a storm in August; others stop locally in September. The observations remain subject to regional acceptance.

## Common Kestrel · 2008

The season is good, with extended passage and two main waves in late September and early October. The first summer observation probably reflects dispersal rather than migration.

## Eurasian Hobby · 2008

Passage is concentrated around mid-September.

## Peregrine Falcon · 2008

The birds observed are probably wandering individuals rather than true migrants.

## Merlin · 2008

## Woodlark · 2009

After the excellent 2008 season, passage returns to a more usual level. Several waves follow in October, with a final early-November resumption.

## Black-crowned Night Heron · 2009
